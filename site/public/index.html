LESS SETUP. MORE SHARING.

Three steps. One local connection.

No cloud upload in the middle.
Just your devices, on your network.

01 /
▣ ↗

Start on one device

Run Sharable on your laptop or use the early Android build. Source and build instructions are on GitHub.

02 /
◉ ⌁

Get on the same Wi-Fi

Join the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Open the address shown by Sharable on the other device and enter the PIN.

03 /
▤ ✓

Send it directly

Files move across your local network in small, checked pieces. Interrupted uploads can resume.
