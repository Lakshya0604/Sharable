Send big files over your own Wi-Fi

Run Sharable on a laptop or Android phone, join the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, and move files directly between devices. Files stay on your devices. They are not uploaded to this website.

Create account Get the app (GitHub)

This website does not transfer files. It is a portal for your account and the app downloads. Transfers happen on the local network between the Sharable apps and work without internet once the app is installed.

Laptop to phone and back

Start Sharable on the laptop, open the address on the phone, enter the PIN.

Built for big files

Files stream in small checked pieces, so memory stays low. Interrupted uploads can resume. Real Wi-Fi speed and 10 GB on a phone are not yet measured.
